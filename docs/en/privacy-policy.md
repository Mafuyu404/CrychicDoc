# Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 10, 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to CrychicDoc (the “Website”), maintained by PickAID. The Website is available at <https://docs.variedmc.cc>. It explains how we collect, use, retain, and delete information.

## Information we collect

- **Access logs:** Our hosting provider may record IP address, browser type, access time, and request URL for security, troubleshooting, and performance monitoring.
- **Information you provide:** If you contact us or submit an issue through GitHub, we receive the account information and message you choose to submit or make public there.
The Website does not offer Pinterest login, account connection, Pin publishing, or Board management, and does not receive or store Pinterest account tokens. PickAID may use Pinterest as an external source of materials or as part of its workflow when producing and promoting videos about this Website. That activity takes place outside the Website; Pinterest handles related data under its own Privacy Policy.

## Cookies and third-party services

The Website does not require an account and does not use advertising-tracking cookies. External services such as VitePress, GitHub, Discord, QQ, Algolia, and Pinterest may process data under their own policies when you follow a link or use their services.

## Retention and deletion

If you contact us through GitHub, we retain the message only as long as needed to handle the request. You can request deletion through the [CrychicDoc GitHub repository](https://github.com/PickAID/CrychicDoc); we will delete information under our control after receiving a request, except where retention is required by law.

## Security

We use reasonable safeguards, including access controls, least-privilege permissions, and encrypted transport. No internet transmission or third-party platform can be guaranteed completely secure. Where required by law, we will notify you of a security incident affecting your information.

## Children’s privacy

The Website is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information.

## Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the service, law, or data practices change. The current version will always be published on this page with an updated date.

## Contact

For privacy questions or deletion requests, open an Issue in the [CrychicDoc GitHub repository](https://github.com/PickAID/CrychicDoc) with “Privacy request” in the title.
